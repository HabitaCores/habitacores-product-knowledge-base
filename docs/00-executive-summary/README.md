## Product Overview

Habitacores is a multi-tenant real-estate operating ecosystem designed to connect property ownership, residential leasing, community operations, resident services, legal workflows, maintenance, payments, and future property development within one unified platform.

Habitacores is not intended to function merely as a property-listing website. It is being designed as the digital operating infrastructure for residential communities and the stakeholders who participate in their complete lifecycle.

## Initial Market

Habitacores will launch initially in Nigeria.

The platform will be designed around Nigerian residential property practices, including:

- Annual rent structures
- Upfront rental and associated charges
- Property inspection workflows
- Estate-agent participation
- Tenancy-agreement preparation
- Resident screening
- Community-based property operations
- Local payment and communication practices

The architecture must support future international expansion without weakening the product's initial Nigerian-market fit.

## Initial Pilot Community

The first implementation will be validated using a real residential property owned by the founding team.

The initial development currently includes:

- Block A
- Six residential units
- A functional masjid within the property perimeter
- Planned construction of an additional block after the completion of Block A

This property will serve as the first controlled operating environment for validating listing, inspection, application, leasing, payment, move-in, resident-management, maintenance, renewal, and future development workflows.

## Product Vision

Habitacores will become a connected operating ecosystem for modern residential property relationships.

The long-term platform will support:

- Property owners
- Property developers
- Property managers
- Residents
- Prospective residents
- Estate agents
- Estate lawyers
- Maintenance vendors
- Financial-service partners
- Insurance partners
- Regulatory and institutional stakeholders

## Mission

To simplify and modernize residential property ownership, leasing, and community operations through a unified, transparent, secure, and locally relevant digital platform.

## Core Product Philosophy

Habitacores will follow these foundational principles:

1. The community is the primary operating context.
2. The property, block, and unit hierarchy must reflect real-world development structures.
3. Annual rent and Nigerian leasing practices must be first-class product concepts.
4. Every material transaction and decision must be transparent and auditable.
5. Users must receive role-specific experiences based on their responsibilities.
6. The platform must scale from six units to large multi-property portfolios.
7. Artificial intelligence must support human decisions, not remove human accountability.
8. Product implementation must follow approved specifications and recorded architectural decisions.
9. Real operational needs must drive software behavior.
10. Security, privacy, and tenant-data isolation must be designed into the platform from the beginning.

## Initial Product Scope

The initial MVP will focus on the operational lifecycle of the founding team's property.

The MVP is expected to support:

- Community registration
- Block and unit management
- Property-listing publication
- Media and amenity presentation
- Prospective-resident inquiries
- Inspection scheduling
- Applicant information collection
- Application review
- Annual rent and fee disclosure
- Applicant approval
- Tenancy-agreement workflow
- Payment-status recording
- Move-in administration
- Resident records
- Maintenance requests
- Lease-renewal tracking
- Internal audit history

The final MVP scope will be defined in the approved Product Requirements and Development Roadmap documents.

## Product Boundaries

Habitacores is not being defined as only:

- A property-listing marketplace
- A rent-collection application
- An estate-agent CRM
- A maintenance-ticketing system
- A legal-document generator
- A construction-management tool
- An accounting application

These may exist as modules or capabilities, but Habitacores is the platform that connects them through a shared identity, shared property model, shared workflows, and shared data architecture.
